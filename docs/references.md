# References: behaviour to aspire to

Clips from other games and real footage of how things should break, fall and move, each tied to a catalogue entry
([catalogue.md](catalogue.md)). The practice (the owner's idea, 2026-10-02):
- **Links only** in this file, with timestamps and what to match. The clips are other people's work.
- **Frames** grabbed for comparison stay in `refs/` (gitignored, personal reference, never committed).
  `tools/ref-frames.ps1` grabs a clip's frames at given times and sets them beside the entry's sandbox contact sheet,
  so an agent, which cannot watch video, can say what differs.
- **Behaviour only.** References for the look and animation wait for milestone 21.

An entry is a candidate until the owner has picked the clip and the moments in it: "candidate" below means found by an
agent's web search on 2026-10-02 and not yet vetted.
